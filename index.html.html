🎯 Purpose of CrimWise

CrimWise was created as a digital learning companion for Criminology students. Its purpose is to organize reviewers, legal studies, PNP information, quizzes, board-exam-style practice and academic tools in one accessible application.

📚 Main Features

  • Student registration and login
  • Subject-based digital reviewers
  • Situational and difficult quizzes
  • Board-exam practice
  • Laws and Legal Studies
  • PNP ranks and history
  • GWA calculator
  • Student profile
  • Local account storage

💡 Why CrimWise Was Created

CrimWise was created to provide Criminology students with one digital place where they can review their subjects, study important laws, learn about the Philippine National Police, practice difficult questions and prepare for future board examinations. Instead of using separate notes, reviewers and tools, the application aims to organize these learning materials into a single student-friendly platform.

🛠️ Development Concept

The application was designed with the goal of being simple to use while still providing useful academic content. The interface, student profile, quizzes, reviewers, calculator and study sections are organized specifically around the needs of Criminology students.
